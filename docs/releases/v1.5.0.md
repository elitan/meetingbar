# MeetingBar 1.5.0

A quieter Graphite interface and clear countdowns for automatic recording.

## What's new

- **Graphite design:** dark neutral surfaces, clearer buttons, compact settings and recording prompts, and a flatter transcript layout across the native app.
- **Playback stays within reach:** the audio player remains at the bottom of the transcript while you scroll. Audio preparation still starts only when you press Play.
- **Stable library navigation:** sidebar actions have reserved space so hovering does not move or wrap titles. Switching meetings or settings sections starts the new view at the top.
- **Automatic start countdown:** after a supported meeting app uses its microphone for 1.5 seconds, the centered reminder counts down for 15 seconds before recording. Choose **Start Now** or **Not Now**; leaving the call cancels the pending start.
- **Automatic stop countdown:** after a call stops using the microphone for one minute, a 30-second countdown offers **Stop Now** or **Keep Recording**. A resumed call cancels the countdown. Five minutes of silence can also trigger forgotten-recording protection.
- **Updated README:** a real native transcript-view render uses fictional content, with an isolated, reproducible screenshot test. Design explorations and their prompts are included separately.

## Privacy and compatibility

Local audio continues to stay on your Mac until you delete its meeting. Existing transcription-provider choices, credentials, microphone preferences, and recordings are preserved. Detection uses microphone-usage metadata, not calendar access; browser detection identifies the browser, not the specific meeting tab.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. This is a source release, with no notarized downloadable app attached. The personal local build uses the existing stable bundle identifier and signing requirement.

## Validation

The regression suite passed: 114 tests passed, 5 opt-in real-model tests skipped, zero failures. The arm64 Release build passed. Native computer-use review covered the library at compact and wide sizes, meeting navigation, persistent playback controls, search, all settings sections, onboarding, and a cancelled delete confirmation. Countdown layouts were checked through native renderer tests.

The menu-bar popover and true pointer-hover interactions were reviewed in source but not fully exercised through computer use. The microphone/call hardware matrix and real-model benchmarks were not rerun for this UI release.
